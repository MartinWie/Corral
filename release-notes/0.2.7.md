Blank terminals repair themselves, and Corral does less work in the background.

- A terminal that comes up blank after switching, waking or unlocking is detected and redrawn on its own
- Terminals you switched away from let go of their last frame after a few seconds, about 17 MB each on a Retina window
- Claude background jobs no longer start `claude agents` every few seconds while they work
- Search indexing reads only the Claude transcripts that changed, and only the new part of an active OpenCode session
- Settings › General › Diagnostics (off by default) logs terminal repairs to Console and turns on `corral debug`

Corral follows macOS light and dark mode, and folders made by agents tidy themselves up.

- With no theme in your Ghostty config, terminals and the sidebar turn light in light mode; dark mode looks as before
- Switching light/dark while Corral runs now reaches every open terminal, also with your own light/dark Ghostty themes
- Fixed the sidebar sometimes taking the light colors of a light/dark theme in dark mode
- Folders made by `corral spawn` and `corral folder` are removed once their last session closes or moves out
- The keyboard shortcuts popup has its side margins back, and shows ⌘ß instead of ⌘SS on German keyboards
